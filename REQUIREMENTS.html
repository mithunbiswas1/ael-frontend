1. Executive Summary & Core Objectives

The primary mandate of this platform is to build a high-performance, secure digital infrastructure dedicated to raising nationwide LPG safety awareness across Bangladesh, standardizing stakeholder guidelines, and providing accredited digital certifications.

  • Safety & Awareness Portal: Direct access to safety norms for domestic consumers, commercial retailers, distributors, auto-gas station operators, and industrial plants.
  • Subscription & Regulatory Information Hub: Real-time incident logs, inquiry reports, safety directives, and regulatory circulars from BERC, LOAB, DoE, and Fire Service & Civil Defense.
  • Accredited LMS & Certification: Comprehensive learning management system hosting modular video lessons, automated quiz generation from pre-set question banks, and QR-verifiable PDF certificates.
  • Revenue & Monetization Channels: Multi-tiered subscriber memberships, commercial advertisement slots, and payment gateway automation (SSLCommerz, bKash, Nagad, Cards).
  • Enterprise Communication: Targeted bulk SMS (Unicode Bangla support) and bulk email campaign managers.

4. Learning Management System (LMS) & Quiz Engine

Course Architecture

Category (Consumer / Retail / Industrial)
└── Course (Metadata, Thumbnail, Price [Free/Paid], Certificate Eligibility)
    └── Module / Section (e.g. "Module 1: Basic Cylinder Mechanics")
        └── Lesson (Hosted Video URL [S3/Vimeo], Duration, Supplementary Notes)
            └── Randomized Quiz (Pre-set question pool, Anti-cheat shuffle)
                └── PDF Digital Certificate (Unique ID & Verification QR)

Learning & Evaluation Workflow

  1. Catalog & Preview: Users browse courses; introductory lecture available without purchase.
  2. Enrollment: Instant activation for Free courses; SSLCommerz checkout for Paid tiers.
  3. Interactive Learning Player: Course syllabus sidebar, resume playback from last saved timestamp, automatic progress synchronization every 10 seconds.
  4. Quiz Gate: Unlocks strictly upon completing $\ge 85\%$ of all course lessons.
  5. Exam Session: Shuffled questions from course question bank (MCQ single/multi-select, True/False), optional countdown timer, anti-cheat restriction (no previewing answers during attempt).
  6. Instant Digital Certification: Automatic generation of tamper-proof PDF certificate upon scoring $\ge$ pass threshold. Each certificate includes learner's name, completion date, unique serial number, and a verifiable QR code.
